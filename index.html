Privacy Policy for Planexi AI

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Planexi AI is a personal planning application for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how Planexi AI handles user information.

Information stored on your device

Planexi AI stores planner information locally on your device, including tasks, shopping items, expense entries, selected currency, schedules, reminder settings and app preferences.

Voice input

Planexi AI can use the speech-recognition service available on your Android device. Depending on your device and speech-recognition provider, audio or speech data may be processed by that provider under its own privacy terms.

AI planning

AI planning is optional and runs only when you request it. To create an AI-generated plan, Planexi AI may send relevant planner context to the Planexi AI backend, including:

  • open task titles, due dates, optional task times and recurrence settings;
  • open shopping items;
  • expense descriptions and amounts included in the planning context;
  • selected currency;
  • device/app language and current date/time information;
  • an anonymous app-generated client identifier used for request limiting and abuse prevention.

The Planexi AI backend sends relevant planning context to OpenAI to generate the requested plan. The OpenAI API key is stored on the backend and is not stored in the Android app.

Please do not place highly sensitive personal information in task, shopping or expense text that you do not want processed when using AI planning.

Data sharing and service providers

When you use AI planning, relevant planner context is processed by service providers used to operate the Planexi AI backend and by OpenAI for the purpose of generating the requested plan. Voice input may be processed by the speech-recognition provider configured on your Android device.

Data retention and deletion

Planexi AI does not intentionally store your task, shopping or expense content in its own application database on the backend. Service providers may maintain security, abuse-prevention or operational logs according to their own retention practices.

You can delete planner content from within the app. Because the current app does not provide user accounts, there is no server-side account to delete. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored app data from the device, subject to Android backup/restore settings.

Advertising and analytics

The current release does not include advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs. If advertising, analytics or additional monetization services are added in a future release, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure will be updated before that release.

Security

Planexi AI uses HTTPS when communicating with its AI backend. Reasonable measures are used to protect information in transit, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Planexi AI is a general productivity application and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when Planexi AI features, service providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will remain available at this URL.
